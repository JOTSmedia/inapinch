IN A PINCH helps you find a bathroom in Key West, with the practical details all in one place.

Public spots + local favorites.

Explore documented bathrooms and bars or restaurants where a customer bathroom is likely. “Bathroom unconfirmed” means availability still needs checking. Purchase rules can vary; ask staff first. Every listing links to its sources.

A pin gets you close.

Some pins identify a park or venue rather than the restroom entrance. Check the location notes and follow signs when you arrive.

Hours need a little context.

“Open by schedule” uses posted hours in Key West’s local time, America/New_York. It is not a live confirmation of access, closures, or occupancy.

Your location is your call.

Location is requested only when you choose “Find near me” or “Nearest first.” It is used in this page, not stored by the app. Saved spots stay in this browser. Directions open in Google Maps.

Your visit. Your verdict.

Leave an anonymous 1–5 star bathroom rating. No account or email needed. A browser cookie lets you update or remove your vote, and everyone sees the shared average.
